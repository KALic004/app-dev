### **Birds of Valhalla**
This series features two humanoid birds: a white raven named **Hugin**, who is friendly, upbeat, and adventurous; and a black raven named **Munin**, who is apathetic, defiant, and kind of a downer. They've been stranded on a mysterious island for who knows how long, and neither of them remembers how they ended up there. To find a way off, they have been exploring the island, looking for clues or something new. After a while of just going in circles, Munin is getting tired, while Hugin remains just as energetic. Because they are complete opposites, they don't get along at times, but despite their conflicts, they have nobody else but each other on the island.

This dynamic changes when Hugin excitedly spots a new character washed up on the shore. The newcomer is completely amnesiac, unable to recall their identity, what happened to them, or how they arrived on the island, just like the ravens. Hugin names the new character **Draugr**. Munin then suggests that Draugr explore the island with Hugin. Hugin immediately objects, noting that they've gone around the island hundreds of times, but Munin remains eager to discover something new with this fresh dynamic.
